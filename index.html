Straight answers.

The important commercial and operational details for brands and creators, before a partnership starts.

What does the 20% commission apply to?

The agency commission is 20% of the agreed creator deal value when that deal is completed. Any creator fees, product, shipping, paid media, production or platform costs remain separate.

Are there retainers or upfront agency fees?

No retainers. The agency model is 20% commission on completed creator deals only. Campaign costs such as creator fees, products, shipping or paid media remain separate.

Do you guarantee reach, sales, virality or creator income?

No. Campaigns and creator offers can be planned and improved, but reach, engagement, deals, income and commercial outcomes cannot be guaranteed. We focus on fit, credible outreach and clean execution.

Can you work across countries and niches?

Yes. Discovery can be global or market-specific and can span all categories. The brief determines language, geography and creator criteria.

Can you contact creators for a brand?

Yes. Outreach can run through relevant email and Instagram channels with personalized messaging, sensible volume and respect for opt-outs and platform limits.

What does a brand need to provide?

A clear product, campaign objective, target audience, working budget range, desired timeline, deliverables and any non-negotiable brand or legal requirements.

How can a creator be considered?

Creators can send a truthful profile through the pitch builder, including niche, audience, market, current performance context and a collaboration idea. Submission does not guarantee selection or a deal.

Does the rate planner tell creators what brands will pay?

No. It calculates a planning range from the creator’s own base fee, selected uplifts and costs. Final rates depend on scope, rights, usage, exclusivity, timing, fit and negotiation.

Do you manage contracts or legal approval?

We can help organize deal terms and the handoff, but brands and creators should review and approve contracts, claims, usage rights, disclosures and legal obligations with qualified advisers.
